# VideoEditing

Turning the user's raw OBS recordings into publishable videos, through cuts an
agent proposes and the user approves.

## Language

**Video**:
One publishable piece, made from one or more recordings, that becomes one
upload.
_Avoid_: project, clip

**Recording** (_registrazione_):
One file produced by OBS: the raw material of a video.
_Avoid_: take, ripresa, footage

**Scene** (_scena_):
A part of a video planned as a unit, often recorded as its own recording.
_Avoid_: segment, part

**Cut list** (_lista dei tagli_):
The decision, for every stretch of a video's recordings, to keep it, cut it, or
speed it up.
_Avoid_: EDL, timeline

**Optional cut** (_taglio facoltativo_):
A cut the agent proposes without being sure of it; it is made only if the user
asks for it.
_Avoid_: minor cut, secondary cut

**Edit** (_montaggio_):
The MP4 rendered from a video's cut list.
_Avoid_: render (as a noun), final cut, export

**Join** (_giunzione_):
A point in the edit where one stretch of the cut list ends and the next one
begins.
_Avoid_: splice, cut point

**Retake** (_ripartenza_):
A sentence the speaker restarts right after getting it wrong. Explaining a
point again later is not a retake.
_Avoid_: repetition

**Playbook** (_manuale_):
The written guidance on making a decent YouTube video, spoken text included,
that the agent applies to its feedback, cut proposals, and thumbnails.
_Avoid_: guidelines, linee guida (YouTube's Community Guidelines are something
else)

**Wait** (_attesa_):
A silent stretch in which the speaker waits for something happening on screen,
typically the AI at work.
_Avoid_: pause (a short silence inside speech), dead air, vuoto

**Marker** (_marcatore_):
A point the user marks in a recording with an OBS hotkey, right after getting a
sentence wrong.
_Avoid_: chapter, chapter marker (chapters are the sections listed in the
YouTube description)

**Compact transcript** (_trascrizione compatta_):
The agent's reading view of a recording: one line per sentence, with its times
and the pause before it.
_Avoid_: packed transcript

**Reference photo** (_foto di riferimento_):
The user's portrait, kept in the channel folder, from which their face in
thumbnails is drawn.
_Avoid_: avatar, profile picture

**Channel template** (_modello del canale_):
The fixed style of every thumbnail: font, colours, and layout.
_Avoid_: theme, brand kit

**Video script** (_copione_):
The plan of what to say and show in a video, written before recording.
_Avoid_: script (in this project, a script is a Python program), outline
